# Legacy results

Previous factorial, shared-threshold, logistic-regression convergence,
GPU-calibration, and earlier sensitivity analyses remain in the historical
parent repository.

They are not part of the primary article estimand.

Effects around +5.5 to +8.5 percentage points were produced by analyses
with unequal realized referral counts and must not be interpreted as the
corrected fixed-model equal-budget effect.

The canonical CDC 2023 effects at the 10% absolute budget are:

- preterm: +2.58 percentage points;
- NICU: +2.73 percentage points;
- low birth weight: +3.43 percentage points.
